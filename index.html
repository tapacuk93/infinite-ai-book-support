FAQ

How do I start a book?

On first launch the app shows what is sent where (see the Privacy Policy) and asks you to agree before the first book is written. Then type any topic — a period of history, a science, a city, a craft — or pick one of the suggestions on the library shelf. The book plans its chapters and starts writing the first one.

How does the endless book work?

The book is an outline that keeps growing. Chapters are planned up front; each chapter is divided into sections when you reach it, and each section into its own subsections, without end.

  • Next reads straight through, breadth-first: every chapter before any chapter's sections, every section at one depth before anything deeper. A short read covers the whole width of a topic; a long one keeps going down.
  • Go deeper dives into the current section's own subsections instead.
  • Back retraces your steps.
  • Contents shows the outline (you can divide any section from there) or a radial map of the whole book.

The library keeps every book you're reading, how many pages you've read (counted at 250 words a page), and the section you're on, so you can pick each one up where you left off.

Pages or scroll?

Either. In Settings → Reading → Layout, Pages turns a section page by page, like an e-reader; Scroll lays it out as one long column. The page is hyphenated and justified, with themes (Automatic, Paper, Sepia, Night), a serif or sans-serif typeface, and adjustable text size. Formulas are typeset properly, and pictures are drawn for a section when it asks for one.

Which languages can I read in?

About 100. Choose the language in Settings → Reading, and switch at any time, even in the middle of a book: section titles are translated, and sections already written are translated from their existing text, so it stays the same book — and you stay at the same place in it. Sections not yet written are written in the new language.

Which AI writes the book?

You choose in Settings → AI → Written by. The default, Consortium, has several models write and review each section together: the most careful text, but a section can take minutes rather than seconds and costs more. Automatic sends each section to whichever model is rated best for its subject. You can also pick one: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral, or Kimi. If the one you picked is unavailable, another model writes the section instead. Outlines, translations, and topic suggestions always use a faster model.

Can the book be read aloud?

Yes. Tap listen and the book is read to you, highlighting the paragraph and word being spoken. In Settings → Listening, choose Natural (an ElevenLabs voice, paid from your AICoin balance, with the device's own voice taking over if it fails) or Device voice (free, works offline, and nothing leaves your device). With "Keep reading into the next section" on, it reads straight on into the next section.

What is "writing ahead", and what does it cost?

Sections are written while you read, so the next one is ready when you get there. Settings → Reading sets how many sections ahead to write (0 to 10; 3 by default), and Download ahead in the reader writes the next 10, 25, or 50 sections on request. Sections written ahead are stored with the book and readable offline.

Every section, picture, translation and natural-voice passage is paid for from your AICoin balance — there's no subscription. A call costs from 1 AICoin, more for a longer one, worked out from the AI provider's own usage figures for that call; the consortium costs more than a single model. Writing ahead spends AICoin on sections before you read them, so set it lower (or to 0) if you'd rather pay only for what you open.

Why am I offered a rewrite of a section I've read?

Every section remembers which model wrote it. When you come back to a section you've already read and a newer model now stands behind the same provider, a banner offers to rewrite it — same place in the book, same title, same subsections. Rewrite writes it again (paid like any other section); Keep leaves it as it is, and you won't be asked again until an even newer model arrives.

Do my books sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud account. Books, sections, and pictures sync through iCloud (CloudKit, in your private database); your settings and recent topics sync through iCloud's key-value store; your AICoin wallet follows you through iCloud Keychain, so your balance is the same on every device. Make sure you're signed into iCloud with the same Apple Account on each device, with iCloud Drive and Keychain turned on. We have no access to any of it. You can clear your recent topics from Settings → History.

How do I buy AICoin?

Settings → AICoin → Get AICoin (or tap your balance). AICoin comes in four sizes, sold as in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. Your balance updates as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. AICoin is a consumable: it's spent as the book is written. It's the same AICoin used by our other apps; see aicoin.oeaio.com for how it works.

A purchase didn't arrive, or I want a refund

If a purchase was charged but your balance didn't go up, reopen the app with a network connection — the purchase is re-checked and credited — and if it still hasn't arrived, email us (below) with the date and the amount. Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, not by us (we never see your payment details): request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

A section failed to write — what do I do?

Usually the network dropped, your AICoin balance ran out, or a provider was briefly unavailable. Check your connection and balance, then try again. If it keeps happening, try a different model in Settings → AI, and let us know.

I found a bug or have a feature request — where do I report it?
